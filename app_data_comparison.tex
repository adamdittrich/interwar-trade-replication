RICardo \parencite{dedinger2017} & Long-run bilateral trade records, with reporter, partner, flow direction and source-related metadata across a much wider archive. & Provides broader historical coverage. Present reconstruction & 5,040 reporter--partner--year rows, 21 countries, 1927--1938; 1927--1937 estimation; separate export/import declarations and world totals. & Supports closed core-plus-external portfolios, recorded-support accounting and comparable conditional predictions.
